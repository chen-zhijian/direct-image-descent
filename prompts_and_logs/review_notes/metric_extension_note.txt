EXTENSION TO NONSPLIT COEFFICIENT METRICS
October 24, 2026

The revised article retains Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 and adds Theorem 6.1
and Corollary 6.2. All new statements keep the product complex geometry,
the product Kahler metric, and the holomorphic external-product line
bundle. Only the Hermitian splitting is removed.

Theorem 6.1(1): no extra assumption in range (ii)

For b=m, q=n, 1<=a<=m and 0<=p<=n, every smooth Hermitian metric h
on L=G box M is allowed. If (L,h) satisfies the total optimal
(a+p,m+n) estimate, then its harmonic direct image satisfies the
optimal (a,m) estimate with the same constant-one auxiliary bound.

The proof retains lifting, solving and descending. The harmonic lift
of a top base antiholomorphic datum has total antiholomorphic degree
m+n, and is automatically closed. The fiber cohomology extraction
is metric-independent because every vertical (p,n)-form is closed.
It can be defined by fixed Serre-dual fiber integrals, commutes with
distributional horizontal differentiation, and annihilates vertically
exact terms. Its contraction property is the minimum-norm property of
harmonic representatives. No false chain identity for the moving
harmonic lift in lower base degrees is asserted.

Theorem 6.1(2): a quantitative condition in range (i)

Let P be harmonic projection, Q=1-P, D the horizontal Chern connection,
B_i=QD_iP, C_i=QDbar_iP, and

  kappa = sum_i (||B_i||_HS^2 + ||C_i||_HS^2).

Let mu(y) be the minimum eigenvalue over the fiber of the TOTAL
curvature commutator in degree (a+p,b+q). It is nonnegative by the
assumed optimal estimate and Watanabe's smooth characterization.
Let e(y) be the minimum eigenvalue, on the harmonic fiber space, of
the operator with quadratic form

  ||D'_Z alpha||^2 + ||(D'_Z)* alpha||^2.

The pointwise sufficient condition is

  mu(y) + e(y) >= 2 m^2 kappa(y).

The total-curvature compression and fiber Bochner identity give
K >= (mu+e) Id for the compressed horizontal curvature commutator.
The Gauss identity gives

  R^H_i barj = P R^L_i barj P - B_j* B_i + C_i* C_j.

The commutator of its correction has operator norm at most
2 m^2 kappa. Thus the base curvature commutator is nonnegative and
the complete-Kahler estimate yields the desired constant-one bound.
The total margin mu matters: e can vanish, and strict total curvature
can still absorb small harmonic variation. Neither necessity nor
sharpness of this sufficient condition is claimed.

Corollary 6.2: mixed-curvature test

Write h=h0 exp(-psi) with h0 a reference product metric. Set
T_i=[dbar_Z,D_i]=-(dbar_Z psi_i) wedge. Then

  B_i=dbar_Z* G_(q+1) T_i P,
  C_i=dbar_Z G_(q-1) T_i* P.

Positive spectral lower bounds in the adjacent fiber degrees bound
kappa by the squared Hilbert-Schmidt norms of these mixed-curvature
operators divided by the corresponding spectral gaps. Substitution
in the theorem gives a sufficient condition stated entirely in terms
of coefficient curvature, harmonic compression and fiber spectra.
Endpoint terms are omitted. No uniform global spectral gap is needed.

Nonvacuity and limitations

The article gives h_t=exp(-|y|^2-t rho(y)chi(z)) on C^2 times P^1,
with (a,b,p,q)=(2,1,1,1). For sufficiently small nonzero t, total
commutator positivity persists and kappa=O(t^2). The harmonic
representative is e^(t rho chi) omega_Z / integral e^(t rho chi),
so its variation is explicit and nonzero. The Gaussian base metric
is fixed; there is no increasingly large pullback twist.

The extension does not remove the product geometry or establish an
unconditional result in range (i). Semipositivity of the line metric
alone is not asserted to imply the quantitative bound. The curvature
and Green identities are established Hodge-theoretic mechanisms;
the article attributes their general antecedents and makes no
unsupported priority claim.

Separate mathematical checks

The weak top-degree cohomology projection, its maximal-domain identity,
the varying Gram-matrix inverse-energy equality, Gauss signs,
mixed-curvature compression, Green-operator signs, spectral factors,
endpoint cases and the example were independently checked after
the new section was written. Minor clarifications about the auxiliary
bundle, L2 density, regular zero in the example, and Green kernels
were incorporated.
