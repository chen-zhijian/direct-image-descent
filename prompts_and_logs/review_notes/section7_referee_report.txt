FOCUSED REFEREE REVIEW: THEOREMS 7.1 AND 7.3
October 24, 2026

Verdict and scope

The statements of Theorems 7.1 and 7.3 are retained under their full
existing hypotheses. This review found a false comparison of geometric
hypotheses and an unstated endpoint restriction, and expanded several
compressed proof steps. It found no counterexample or theorem-level
gap requiring an additional hypothesis. This is a focused mathematical
audit, not certification of novelty or an exhaustive review of every
result elsewhere in the article.

Findings, ordered by significance

1. Incorrect comparison with Kodaira--Spencer class vanishing.

The introduction to Theorem 7.3 said that pointwise annihilation of
the Serre-dual sections was stronger than vanishing of a Kodaira--Spencer
class. That implication is false: for p=n, the dual sections have
degree zero and annihilation is automatic, including in the article's
nonisotrivial elliptic example with nonzero Kodaira--Spencer class.

The revised article requires pointwise vanishing of the insertions,
rather than merely vanishing of their cohomology classes or harmonic
projections. It expressly does not require the Kodaira--Spencer class
itself to vanish. Conversely, a zero Kodaira--Spencer class does not
force the insertion of the distinguished representative for the chosen
horizontal metric to vanish pointwise. The accompanying extension
note and synopsis are corrected consistently.

2. Chain identity endpoint was not stated.

Pi_j was defined only for 0<=j<=m, but the displayed chain identity
mentioned Pi_(j+1) without a range. The revision states 0<=j<m. This
is exactly the range used to descend the solution Pi_(m-1)U.

3. Curvature comparison needed a more explicit quadratic-form argument.

The original argument is valid, but a reader could mistake the
fiber-dependent inverse-square-root map S_x for a differential lift
and consequently doubt the use of harmonicity. The revision defines
the pointwise exterior-power embedding I_x and proves its exact
diagonal curvature-block identity. With a fixed orthonormal base-form
frame and harmonic vertical coefficients, integration gives the
horizontal curvature pairing minus the fiber Bochner energy.

No derivative of I_x or S_x is taken. Mixed coefficient curvature
changes horizontal/vertical degree and has zero diagonal compression.
The integral uses the fiber measure, with no total horizontal volume
factor. The delta estimate is stated as an explicit quadratic-form
bound after harmonic compression. The original budget and constant
2 m^2 remain valid.

4. Kodaira--Spencer insertion signs and projected connection.

The revision defines J_i=(A_i^alpha_barbeta dbar z^beta wedge)iota_alpha
and the conjugate insertion explicitly. Kahler symmetry of the lowered
tensor gives bar J_i=J_i^*. Taking the fixed-type block of the full
covariant-Lie commutator produces -J_i bar J_j+bar J_j J_i. Harmonic
compression then gives exactly +K_j^*K_i-bar K_i^*bar K_j; orthogonal
Gauss projection adds -B_j^*B_i+C_i^*C_j.

The relative Dolbeault homotopy
 D_bar_i=partial_bar_yi+dbar_y iota_bar_a_i+iota_bar_a_i dbar_y
is displayed, proving that P D^(0,1) induces the direct-image
holomorphic structure. This does not claim that D_bar_i preserves
harmonic representatives. The fiber metric-compatibility identity is
also displayed. Comparison with Choi--Schumacher is made in a
holomorphic H-normal frame, fixing the derivative-term interpretation.

5. Theorem 7.3: exact orthogonal extension and chain contraction.

The revision displays the complete obstruction formula
 dbar(hat s)=sum dy^i wedge hat(A_i contraction s)
   -sum dy^i wedge dbar y^j wedge iota_(W_i barj^(1,0))(hat s).
The adapted horizontal metric kills W, and pointwise annihilation
kills the first term. Holomorphic coefficients in a holomorphic L^*
frame contribute no additional derivative term.

The Serre pairing is normalized explicitly by integral alpha wedge s,
using the same order as fiber integration. The projection is then
exactly componentwise vertical cohomology extraction; under the Hodge
identification it is harmonic projection. This gives Pi_j J_j=Id
without a hidden phase and proves contraction without assuming an
extra isometry on the Serre-dual bundle. Adaptedness gives
dV_X=dV_Y dV_y for normalized volumes, with no binomial factor.

Local holomorphic dual frames and compactly supported base tests
justify the distributional identity. Properness supplies compact
support upstairs. The contraction proves the maximal-domain claim;
no global bounds on derivatives of harmonic frames are needed.

6. Auxiliary curvature kernels and optimal estimates.

The revised proof diagonalizes the positive auxiliary curvature on
the base. In total top antiholomorphic degree its pullback commutator
has eigenvalues given by sums over holomorphic horizontal indices.
On the lifted (a,p) block these are exactly the base eigenvalues,
strictly positive because a>=1. The extended quadratic form q_(B_X)
is used in the inverse-energy identity, avoiding an apparent inverse
of an operator that can have a kernel on other blocks.

Source and novelty checks

Watanabe's arXiv:2109.12554v2, Definitions 1.3--1.4 and Theorems
1.5--1.6, support the fixed-metric extremal characterization with the
same auxiliary-bundle and compact-support quantifiers. Completeness
supplies the converse and the semidefinite estimate used here.

Choi--Schumacher, arXiv:2407.04298v1, formula (7.2) and the horizontal
bracket calculation in Lemma 8.1, apply to the prescribed ordinary
harmonic metric with arbitrary smooth Hermitian coefficients. The
manuscript attributes these identities and asserts no new general
curvature formula, unrestricted descent, sharpness, or priority.
This review does not establish originality of the sufficient conditions.

The nonisotrivial elliptic trace example and the negative R^1 pi_*O
counterexample were recomputed. They support the stated limitations;
neither requires a pullback twist of the flat coefficient bundle.

Validation

Separate mathematical checks covered Theorem 7.1, Theorem 7.3, and
their source/analytic inputs. The new proof passages were checked
again after revision. The revised article compiles to 27 pages with
no LaTeX warnings or overfull boxes. Reference checks found no missing
or duplicate labels or missing citations; all page renders were
inspected, with the revised proof pages also inspected at full size.
Theorems 1.1, 1.2, 6.1, 7.1, and 7.3 retain their numbering. The
original dualN_ai.tex is not changed, and dated backups preserve the
pre-review article.
