SUMMARY OF THE SOURCE MANUSCRIPT AND THE NEW ARTICLE

The source manuscript dualN_ai.tex establishes or records six main points:

1. Under infinitesimal triviality and constant top cohomology dimension,
   a smooth dual Nakano semipositive coefficient bundle has a smooth
   dual Nakano semipositive top direct image R^n pi_* E.
2. The top (0,n) L2 density and harmonic representative are independent
   of the auxiliary fiber metric. Relative Serre duality is an isometry
   with the dual of the canonical adjoint direct image.
3. Vanishing Kodaira--Spencer maps give local products on a dense open
   locus. A negative-adjoint curvature identity proves positivity there,
   and smoothness extends it everywhere. Flatness, scalar lower bounds,
   and numerical nefness follow under their stated hypotheses.
4. General harmonic lifts need not commute with differentiation. A
   product manifold alone is insufficient if the coefficient metric
   varies along the fibers and base together.
5. For arbitrary Hodge degrees, compressed ambient curvature dominating
   the harmonic variation gives a conditional curvature inequality and
   constant-one estimates. This is not automatic from semipositivity.
   Auxiliary scalar tests are impossible in middle base degree.
6. The main curvature mechanism has established antecedents. The
   Mourougane--Takayama metric must be distinguished from the ordinary
   harmonic metric; broad adjoint, pluricanonical, and singular extension
   results are not claimed as consequences.

The new standalone AMS article makes two results its main theorems:

Theorem 1.1 retains the source manuscript's top-direct-image theorem.
Theorem 1.2 is the quoted product descent theorem in the ranges
  (i) a=m, p=n, b>=1; or (ii) b=m, q=n, a>=1,
with the remaining indices in their stated dimension ranges.

The product theorem is proved by smooth curvature characterization,
closed harmonic lifting, an exact inverse-energy identity, a complete
Kahler solution on the product, and contractive harmonic descent.
Its pullback auxiliary bundle is only semipositive; this is handled
through the curvature theorem, not by an invalid direct use of the
positive-auxiliary hypothesis. A second fiber Bochner calculation shares
the same curvature-characterization input and avoids solving upstairs.
A nonzero q=1 example shows that the resulting
degree-specific estimate need not imply semipositive line curvature.

The referee revision of October 23, 2026 retains both main theorem
statements and strengthens the analytic details. A bounded harmonic
chain-retraction lemma gives descent on maximal distributional L2
domains. The complete-Kahler existence proof now spells out the
adjoint-domain argument, and the exact exterior-product sign is fixed.
Theorem 1.2 is explicitly presented as a product corollary of Watanabe's
extremal-degree curvature characterization and compact-fiber Bochner
theory, with no priority, necessity, or sharpness claim. The varying
metric example only obstructs naive harmonic lifting; it does not prove
that metric splitting is necessary for the conclusion.

The extension of October 24, 2026 adds Theorem 6.1 and Corollary 6.2.
The holomorphic product and fixed product Kahler metric are retained,
but the coefficient metric need not split.

- In range (ii), b=m and q=n, arbitrary smooth coefficient metrics are
  allowed. A top-degree harmonic lift is automatically closed, and
  the metric-independent cohomology map descends the weak solution.
- In range (i), a sufficient condition is mu+e >= 2 m^2 kappa, where
  mu is the minimum total curvature-commutator eigenvalue over a fiber,
  e is the minimum fiber Bochner-energy eigenvalue on harmonic forms,
  and kappa is the squared Hilbert-Schmidt size of harmonic variation.
  Compression gives a horizontal curvature lower bound mu+e, while
  the Gauss correction is bounded by 2 m^2 kappa.
- Mixed coefficient curvature and adjacent-degree Green operators
  give a directly usable sufficient bound for kappa.
- A nonsplit metric on C^2 times P^1 has explicitly varying harmonic
  representatives and satisfies the criterion for a small perturbation
  of a fixed Gaussian base metric.

These Section 6 results are sufficient product consequences of known
methods; no sharpness or priority claim is made.

The further fibration extension of October 24, 2026 replaces the short
supporting Section 7 by a full proof for smooth proper Kahler fibrations.
Theorem 7.1 assumes constant cohomology dimension, complete metrics,
positive auxiliary bundles, the total optimal condition, and

  mu+e >= delta+2 m^2(tau+sigma+kappa).

Here sigma measures actual Kodaira--Spencer insertion, tau the compressed
horizontal-bracket term, and delta the discrepancy between horizontal
metrics; kappa, mu, and e extend the Section 6 quantities. Pointwise
metric comparison, fiber Bochner integration, the Choi--Schumacher
curvature formula, and the complete estimate on the base prove the
result. An adapted horizontal metric makes delta=tau=0.

Theorem 7.3 proves exact top-degree lifting, solving, and descending
under an adapted horizontal metric and pointwise Kodaira--Spencer
annihilation of every holomorphic Serre-dual section. Holomorphic
orthogonal extensions give a contractive distributional chain map;
no smallness bound on harmonic variation is needed in this theorem.
The annihilation is automatic for p=n. A nonisotrivial elliptic trace
example satisfies the assumptions with flat coefficients. On the
same family, R^1 pi_*O supplies a counterexample to unconditional
descent, although the total optimal condition holds.

The new section attributes the curvature identities to the existing
literature and makes no necessity, sharpness, or priority claim. It
does not assert unrestricted descent on varying fibrations. See
fibration_extension_note.txt for the assumptions and checks in detail.

The subsequent focused referee revision of Theorems 7.1 and 7.3
retains both theorem statements. It corrects the false suggestion that
annihilation of the Serre-dual sections implies vanishing of the
Kodaira--Spencer class. It adds explicit curvature-compression and
insertion-sign calculations, the full orthogonal-extension obstruction,
a normalized coefficientwise chain projection, and the maximal-domain
and auxiliary-kernel details. See section7_referee_report.txt.
The new main.tex is self-contained; it does not input the old manuscript
or this summary file. The original dualN_ai.tex is preserved.
